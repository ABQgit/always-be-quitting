## Page Structure (base: premium-coaching copy)

1. **Hero** — from premium-coaching: "Premium 1:1 Coaching That Actually Works" + video slot ("Please watch this video…" — pending Jon's decision on which video). Naming: **"The 1:1 Program"** (renamed 2026-08-23 — see item 6).
2. **The expert pitch** — premium-coaching's long-form paragraphs verbatim ("This is not just another quit attempt… The 'magic formula' isn't a formula… I've never seen a perfect quitting attempt… I'm all-in with my clients."), typos fixed ("a proven coaching techniques", "eachother", "you chose").
3. **What to Expect From 1:1 Coaching** — the 8 bullets. **Bodies verbatim; the eight titles were rewritten 2026-08-25 (Jon)** because the originals were category labels ("Fluid quit plan", "Advanced mindset focus"), so scanning the section — which means reading only the titles — returned a taxonomy and none of the reasons to buy. Titles now carry the claim, bodies still carry the substance. One body word changed: "Easy software-based **scheduling**". **All eight cards kept** — the scheduling card stays because flexibility signals a live service rather than "a paint by numbers course", and cards 7/8 stay separate because they run in opposite directions (your life → your quit, then your quit → your life). Two titles carry recorded reasoning and must not be simplified: *"You get me, not a program"* (not "a real person, not an app", which understates Jon's training) and *"Everyone deserves to quit, whatever your life looks like"* (the barrier is believing help is for people who have their act together, not fear of disclosure). **The mindset card features mindset and does not mention willpower at all** — Jon: *"I WANT MINDSET TO BE FEATURED. DON'T EVEN TALK ABOUT WILLPOWER."* Two drafts were rejected getting here: one discounted willpower, and the correction made the card about willpower rather than mindset. The word does not belong on the card in any framing.
4. ~~**Mechanics grid**~~ — **CUT 2026-08-25 (Jon).** Six cards on scheduling, video links and file storage. Three (1:1 Sessions, Email Support, Progress Tracking) were already in the offer card's prose; two (Easy Scheduling, Simple Video Sessions) were friction-removal nobody weighs at $1,200. It also followed the eight "What to Expect" cards, giving the reader fourteen near-identical tiles in a row. **Both sentences of the Quit Date card were moved verbatim into FAQ 2** ("I'm not ready to quit yet") — including *"You can also begin having already started quitting,"* which is the only welcome on the site to someone mid-quit. Nothing was lost.
5. **"Pays for Itself" ROI section** — verbatim EXCEPT "Stop being a slave to nicotine use" → approved swap ("stop feeling controlled by nicotine").
6. **THE OFFER (replaces the two-package section):**
   - **$97 Intro Session first** (entry action): 50 min, once per person ever, **NOT credited toward the Program (reversed 2026-08-14)**. Booking via Cal.com.
   - **The 1:1 Program** (renamed 2026-08-23; was "Quit for the Last Time: The 1:1 Program" — the tagline attached to a price read as an outcome promise)**:** 6 × 50-min over 12 weeks, email access to Jon, $1,200 list. Direct purchase for ready buyers. Add-on sessions ~$150 for alumni.
   - No strikethrough pricing. **Sale windows run as time-boxed Stripe promotion codes applied at checkout (confirmed 2026-08-18)** — never a crossed-out number on the page. Between sales the page shows $1,200, full stop.
7. **Katherine long-form testimonial** — verbatim, **full length, and the only one on the page. All three of those are deliberate (Jon, 2026-08-25).** She is the only client who speaks directly to Jon's ability and to the coaching relationship, and who closes by telling the reader to hire him: *"If you have reservations about investing in a coach, I cannot recommend Jon enough."* That is precisely the case a $1,200 page has to make, made in a buyer's own words. **Do not propose adding testimonials here, trimming this one, or copying the homepage's three-up treatment** — more quotes would dilute the one that does the work. This page carrying less proof than the homepage is not a gap.
8. **Failure-stakes close** — premium-coaching's StoryBrand failure line, "$1k, $2k, $3k, $4k" formatting fixed, "invested in premium coaching" → updated offer name.
9. **FAQ** — objection-handling set (one-per-person rule, **no-credit rule (2026-08-14)**, session vs Program vs community, reschedule policy, nothing-automatic framing). Source copy to be approved by Jon (drafts assembled from his language).
